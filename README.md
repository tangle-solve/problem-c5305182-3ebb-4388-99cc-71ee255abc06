### ALGO-000033: Superlinear unrestricted Boolean circuit lower bounds
- **Subfield:** Boolean circuit and formula complexity
- **Open:** Exhibit an explicit polynomial-time computable Boolean function requiring \(\omega(n)\) gates in unrestricted bounded-fan-in Boolean circuits.
- **Status:** Open for decades despite strong bounds for restricted models. Counting proves that most functions require exponentially many gates, but does not make them efficiently explicit.
- **Type:** impossibility
- **Why:** Would cross a basic barrier toward general circuit lower bounds.
- **Key works:** Shannon (1949), *The Synthesis of Two-Terminal Switching Circuits*; Viola (2017), *Selected Challenges in Computational Lower Bounds*.
- **Related:** Hardware design—provable limits of circuit compression; cryptography—explicit hard functions.
